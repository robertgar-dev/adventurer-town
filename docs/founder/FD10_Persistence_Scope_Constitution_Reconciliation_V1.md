- **D1 — ~20 persist, rest ephemeral:** **CONSISTENT** with §V **R3** ("a small named cast,"

  ("return condition, repeat appearance, recent service outcome, occasional notable Event Feed
  memory"). **"Visibly change over time" is the load-bearing risk** — see caveats C2/C3.

## Three binding caveats the design pass MUST carry

- **C1 — Citation.** Fixed in FD10: §VII reserved; §III/§V/§VI binding. *(Done.)*
- **C2 — Bound "remember."** Memory stays **recent/occasional outcomes**, not a cumulative
  per-adventurer ledger — a full memory record becomes a **per-visitor biography**, which **§VI
  excludes**.
- **C3 — "Visibly change" axes (AMENDED 2026-06-23 by founder override).** **Per-adventurer
  renown is now ADMITTED** by deliberate founder decision, subject to: **(a)** it spills **upward**
  into town renown (a tributary, never a rival); **(b)** it is **never a visible number**; and
  **(c)** **§V legality confirmed** — verified verbatim in step 1 of ratification (R1/R2 affirm;
  no conflicting text). Still **EXCLUDED**:
  - **leveling / stats shown as numbers** → **§VI "Adventurer leveling or stats."**
  - **player-driven relationship systems** → **§VI "Relationship systems."**

  Progression is **witnessed through the world** (gear/adornment, scale of purchases, bearing, how
  the town speaks of them) — **never an RPG sheet or any visible number** (per FD10 D3/D3a). The
  prior blanket exclusion of per-adventurer reputation/standing is **superseded** for renown only,
  under (a)–(c); the town remains the primary beneficiary (§V R1/R2).
